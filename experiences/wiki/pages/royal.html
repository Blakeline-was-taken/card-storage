Profile

Appearance

Sawyer is a ghoul, although this particular one is a mass of bones with a skull placed on top and a huge bite mark in said skull. Despite him not possessing ghost-like traits like the other ghouls, he is very much one, and his body works the same as all the other ghouls, with the added disability in his movement.

Background

Lore

Sawyer was born in 1702, and was eventually eaten by a hungry dog in Fall of 1778 at the age of 76. The bite mark in his skull is a permanent reminder of that traumatic incident, something that Sawyer never quite moved on from. Perhaps due to his old age and lack of mobility, he was not given a role by Grimora, instead simply guarding the undead temple.

Development

Sawyer seems to have been one of the first underlings to be developed for the game. Most of those characters exhibit signs of mental illness, which Sawyer is the only one to clearly show among all the ghouls.

Personality

Sawyer is deeply traumatised by his past, to the point that any form of contact with anyone is terrifying to him. Even as much as looking at his defigured body is enough to make him stress out, and as such he is a difficult character to try and learn more from, as he is constantly fearful of others' actions and words. He is especially triggered by snarling, sniffing and other dog-like behavior.

Boss Battle

Phase 1

A card is played in the backlane every time he is dealt direct damage, which only gets played in his next turn.

Phase Transition

Sawyer snaps and lashes out in fear, destroying all of the Player's cards.

Phase 2

He no longer plays cards upon being dealt direct damage, instead the response is one of his cards attacking immediately. They cannot strike empty spaces this way (otherwise the fight would be impossible).
