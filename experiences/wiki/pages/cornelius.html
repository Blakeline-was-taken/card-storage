Role

Kaycee is the first boss of the Undead Temple. She oversees the [ADD AREA HERE]. After being summoned, Kaycee asks the player that they warm her up, feeling like the cold is unbearable, and shall they succeed, she will give them the epitaph part they seek.

Profile

Appearance

Kaycee is a ghoul, a floating ghost with a skull for a head and a large icicle planted in the top of her skull. She is constantly shivering, as the temperature lowers around her.

Background

Lore

Kaycee was born sometime in 1970, and eventually died in Winter of 1992, killed by a falling piece of ice at the young age of 22. She was given the task of maintaining the well to collect the OLD_DATA by Grimora, though the constant cold made it difficult. The ice embedded in her head is a remnant of her tragic death, and also the reason why she's permanently cold.

Development

This Ghoul is based on one of the developers of Inscryption, one that ironically died due to fire related complications. She was one of the first to discover the twisted nature of Inscryption and of the Karnoffel Code.

Personality

Despite her dialogue being constantly interrupted by her being cold, Kaycee seems like a pretty laid back person, employing relatively casual language. It seems like her personality was calqued on the developer that she was based on. The cold does seem to be almost the only thing she can think about, occupying every thought.

Boss Battle

Phase 1

  • At the end of either battler's turn, a card on that side gets frozen.
  • At the start of the Player's turn, one card on a random side of the board thaws out.

Phase Transition

Kaycee freezes all of the Player's remaining cards.

Phase 2

Similar to Phase 1, except at the end of Kaycee's turn instead of freezing one of her cards, it freezes another one of the Player's cards. Kaycee also now only plays Glaciers, special cards that contain her actual phase 2 cards and drop them upon being destroyed. They are tanky and slowly melt over time.
