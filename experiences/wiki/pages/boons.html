Boons

Boons act as a passive version of Items, where instead of needing to be triggered manually by the player they have different, more subtle ways of activating. They also aren't lost upon being activated, unlike items.

  2. Major Boons

Common Boons

Common boons can be gained by the Player through the Moon Altar event.

Boon of Stones

Terrain cards can be sacrificed.
Boon of the Lord

Gain 2 bones at the start of the battle.
Boon of the Thunder

Start the battle with 1 more energy.
Boon of the Light

Replace a side deck card from the starting hand with a Prism Mox.
Boon of the Hand

Draw 1 more card from either deck at the start of the battle.
Boon of the Mulligan

One more reshuffle of the deck is allowed at the start of the battle.
Boon of Rallying

The first card you kill is added to your hand for this battle.
Boon of Greed

Gain 3 extra foils when winning a battle.
Boon of the Cheater

The hand of the opponent is revealed.
Boon of Knowledge

The Side Deck is always face-up.
Boon the Tactician

The draw step may be skipped to rearrange the positions of your cards.
Boon of Rituals

When playing a card, the scales may be brought closer to you to lower its cost.

(1 blood/gem = 2 spaces, 1 bone/energy = 1 space)
Boon of Thorns

Cards that deal direct damage to you are dealt 1 damage in return.
Boon of the Anchor

Your cards cannot be moved by outside forces.
Boon of Willpower

Your cards cannot lose power during a battle.
Boon of the Lord

Your cards cannot be killed by sigils.
Boon of Synergy

If your field is full of cards that share a characteristic other than temple and rarity, they all gain 1 power.
Boon of the Scholar

Active abilities cost nothing, but can only be triggered once per turn.
Boon of the Hoarder

You may carry one more item.
Boon of the Cartographer

Winning a battle lets you move or swap a tile from the map.

Major Boons

Major boons can be gained by the Player through the Tinkered Moon Altar event.

Boon of the Negotiator

You may try to reason the bosses into going easy on you.
Boon of Time

Your opponent skips their first turn.
Boon of the Ambidextrous

You may draw an extra main deck card during your draw step, but if you do, that card is discarded at the end of your turn if you do not play it.
Boon of Cinders

When one of your cards is killed in combat, it goes back to your hand with 1 less power and health.
Boon of the Eclipse

Your cards ignore negative sigils.
Boon of Authority

Only one card may attack each turn.
Boon of War

Turns have an extra attack step.
Boon of Scales

When the scales reach your candle, you cannot take any more direct damage until your next turn.
Boon of Denial

If the scales are at your skull, you only lose the battle if they still are when it's the opponent's turn. This also works for the opponent.
Boon of the Candle

When losing a battle, this boon is destroyed instead of a candle being blown out.
